Privacy Policy – FileHQ Pin Publisher

Effective: 27 September 2026

This application is a personal-use tool operated by its owner to publish pins to the owner's own Pinterest business account through the Pinterest API.

What data is handled: the app accesses only the owner's Pinterest boards and pins, authorized explicitly by the owner via Pinterest OAuth. It does not collect, store, or share personal data of any other Pinterest user, and it does not sell or share data with third parties.

Credentials: API tokens are stored securely on the owner's private server and are never exposed publicly.

Affiliate links: some pins published through the app contain affiliate links; each such pin carries a clear disclosure (e.g. #ad).
